    Right triangle rules

    • The hypotenuse is across from the 90° angle. It is the longest side, and it never changes.
    • Mark θ first. The opposite side is across from θ, and the adjacent side is the leg next to θ. If you change the angle, they swap.
    • Memory aid: SOH CAH TOA (Sine = Opposite/Hypotenuse, Cosine = Adjacent/Hypotenuse, Tangent = Opposite/Adjacent)
    • sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse, cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan θ = opposite/adjacent.
    • Finding a side: label the side you know and the side you want, pick the ratio that uses both, then solve. A leg is always shorter than the hypotenuse.
    • Finding an angle: use sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹ or tan⁻¹ (2nd, then SIN, COS or TAN). The −1 means undo, not 1 over.
    • Use degree mode. Check it: sin(30) should give 0.5. If you get −0.988, the calculator is in radian mode.

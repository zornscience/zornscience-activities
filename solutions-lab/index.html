Solutions Lab

Instructions

Make a solution, dilute a stock solution, explore solubility curves, then try the challenges.

  1. Tare the balance, weigh solute, transfer, dissolve, fill to the line, then mix.
  2. Measure stock with a pipet or graduated cylinder. Fill to the total final volume.
  3. Heat and cool 100 g of water. Compare the plotted point with the solubility curve.
  4. Record data and use the calculations to explain your results.

2. Read the graph

Solubility curve

Curve values

Below the curve: unsaturated. On the curve (±1 g): saturated. Above the curve: supersaturated.

The point shows the dissolved amount. Solid on the bottom means the solution is saturated. Only dissolved excess without solid makes a supersaturated solution.

## Independent worked answers

Worked before authoring the bank. These are original questions aligned to College Board CED topics 9.1 to 9.11, not official exam items. Values are rounded classroom thermodynamic data at 298 K unless a prompt specifies another temperature. Assume ΔH° and ΔS° are approximately constant over each stated temperature interval. Entropy and reaction energy values are per mole of reaction as written. Ideal dilute solutions approximate activities; K and Q are dimensionless. Standard gas conditions refer to pressure, not automatically to a temperature of 0 °C.

R = 8.314 J/(mol·K). F = 96,485 C/mol e<sup>−</sup>. Convert J to kJ before combining entropy terms with enthalpy. Atomic masses retain the earlier units' two-decimal values; add Cu 63.55, Zn 65.38 and Ag 107.87 g/mol for this unit. Signs are part of every entropy-change, free-energy and potential key. Numeric magnitude checks reject the opposite sign, and each signed numeric explanation explicitly identifies the required sign. A positive number without a typed + is mathematically positive and follows the existing engine's rules. Precision notes are advisory.

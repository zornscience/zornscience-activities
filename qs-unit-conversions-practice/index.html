    Using conversion factors

    • A conversion factor is an equality written as a fraction. It equals 1, so the amount does not change.
    • Each equality gives two factors: 1 min = 60 s gives (60 s / 1 min) and (1 min / 60 s).
    • Put the new unit on top and the starting unit on the bottom, then cross out the units that cancel: 3.25 min × (60 s / 1 min) = 195 s.
    • If nothing cancels (you get s²/min), flip the factor: 150 s × (1 min / 60 s) = 2.5 min.
    • Defined equalities are exact. They never limit significant figures, so the answer keeps the measurement's: 4.25 h × (60 min / 1 h) = 255 min.

    Exact equalities

    • Time: 1 min = 60 s, 1 h = 60 min, 1 d = 24 h
    • Length: 1 yd = 3 ft, 1 ft = 12 in, 1 in = 2.54 cm
    • Symbols: s seconds, min minutes, h hours, d days, yd yards, ft feet, in inches, cm centimeters

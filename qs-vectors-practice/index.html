    Vector rules

    • A scalar has a size only, like 15 kg or 15 m/s. A vector has a magnitude and a direction, like 15 N upward. Speed is a scalar, and velocity is a vector.
    • An arrow shows a vector. Its length shows the magnitude with a scale, like 1 square = 10 N, and it points from its tail to its tip.
    • Tip to tail: the resultant R runs from the first vector's tail to the last vector's tip. The order does not matter.
    • Same direction: add the magnitudes. Opposite directions: subtract, and use the direction of the larger one.
    • Perpendicular: R = √(A² + B²). Find the direction with tan⁻¹ and state it like 53° north of east.
    • Components, with θ measured from the horizontal: Rx = R cos θ and Ry = R sin θ. If θ is measured from the vertical, sin and cos switch.

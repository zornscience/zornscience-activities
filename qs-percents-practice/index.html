    Percent rules

    • Percent means per hundred: 35% = 35/100 = 0.35.
    • Percent to decimal: move the decimal point 2 places left (4.5% = 0.045). Decimal to percent: move it 2 places right.
    • percent = part ÷ whole × 100. The whole always goes on the bottom.
    • part = (percent ÷ 100) × whole. The part has the same units as the whole.
    • Percent composition = mass of the element ÷ mass of the compound × 100. All the percents add up to about 100%.
    • Percent yield = actual yield ÷ theoretical yield × 100.
    • Atomic masses from the lesson: H = 1.01, C = 12.01, N = 14.01, O = 16.00. Questions give any others.

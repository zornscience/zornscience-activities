    Tests for relationships

    • Direct: a straight line through the origin. Double x and y doubles; triple x and y triples.
    • Ratio test: y ÷ x is the same in every row, and that value is the constant (aluminum: 2.7 g/cm³).
    • Inverse: a curve that drops steeply, then flattens out and never touches the axes. Double x and y is cut in half.
    • Product test: x × y is the same in every row (Boyle's law: PV = 400 kPa·L).
    • Linear but not direct: a straight line that does not pass through the origin, like a beaker plus water that starts at 50 g.
    • Squared: a curve that bends upward. Double x and y is multiplied by 4; triple x and y is multiplied by 9.
    • Test by doubling x: see what happens to y.

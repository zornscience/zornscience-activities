    Dimensional analysis

    • Map the path first: write the starting unit and the goal, with one factor per step.
    • Chain the factors so each bottom unit cancels the top unit before it: 1.5 h × (60 min / 1 h) × (60 s / 1 min) = 5400 s.
    • Rates: convert one unit at a time. Hours are on the bottom of km/h, so h goes on top: 90.0 km/h × (1000 m / 1 km) × (1 h / 3600 s) = 25.0 m/s.
    • Squared and cubed units: square or cube the whole factor, numbers and units. (100 cm / 1 m)³ gives 1 m³ = 1,000,000 cm³, and 1 m² = 10,000 cm².
    • 1 cm³ = 1 mL exactly: 0.355 L = 355 mL = 355 cm³.
    • Let the units check the setup: m/s × s gives m, but m/s ÷ s gives m/s², so that setup is wrong.
    • The factors are exact, so the answer keeps the measurement's significant figures. When zeros at the end would be unclear, use scientific notation: 2.50 d = 3.60 × 103 min.

Chemistry labs

Virtual lab benches for gases, solutions, acids and bases, and reaction rates.

  • Chemical bonding

    Chemical Bonding Lab

    Investigate four unknown solids with realistic lab equipment. Compare appearance, conductivity, melting, solubility in water and cyclohexane, and malleability to infer bonding types.

    Virtual lab

  • Properties of elements

    Metals, Nonmetals & Metalloids Lab

    Investigate three unknown solids with a watch glass, mortar and pestle, conductivity tester, and dilute hydrochloric acid. Use physical and chemical evidence to classify each sample.

    Virtual lab

  • Reaction kinetics

    Iodine Clock Lab

    Measure and mix two clear solutions on a realistic lab bench. Explore concentration and temperature, watch the sudden blue endpoint, and try a 20-second challenge.

    Virtual lab

  • Net ionic equations

    Net Ionic Reactions Lab

    Mix ten pairs of reagents in realistic glassware. Observe precipitates, a metal displacement reaction, and mixtures with no visible reaction.

    Virtual lab

  • Atomic emission

    Virtual Flame Test Lab

    Click eight known metal salt samples and an unknown to compare their characteristic flame colors in a Bunsen burner.

    Virtual lab

  • Analytical chemistry

    Gravimetric Analysis

    Identify an unknown carbonate with a realistic lab bench. Precipitate, filter, wash, and dry calcium carbonate to constant mass.

  • Equilibrium

    Equilibrium Sandbox

  • Thermodynamics

  • Beer's Law

ZornScience

Practice Activities

Each activity goes with a lesson on zornscience.org. No login needed, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

Chemistry · Ionic Bonding

Predicting Ion Charge Trainer

Find valence electrons, decide what changes, and check your reasoning with hints and feedback.

Chemistry · Ionic Bonding

Ionic Formula Builder

Turn names into formulas and formulas into names, and check that the charges add to 0.

Chemistry · Chemical Reactions

Reaction Builder

Choose synthesis, decomposition, single replacement, or double replacement, then build products, balance equations, and review your practice.

Chemistry · Reference

Periodic Table

Look up any element, and color the table by atomic radius, electronegativity or ionization energy to see the trends.

Chemistry · Periodic Trends

Periodic Trends Practice

Compare two elements and use the trend to decide which has the larger radius or the higher electronegativity or ionization energy.

Chemistry · Covalent Bonding

Lewis Structure and VSEPR Builder

Count valence electrons, build each Lewis structure, then predict its shape, bond angle and polarity.

Chemistry · Stoichiometry

Stoichiometry Coach

Build conversion factors, watch the units cancel, and solve grams and moles problems with hints and feedback.

Chemistry · Stoichiometry

Limiting Reactant Visualizer

Pick starting moles of two reactants, predict which runs out, and watch the molecules pair up into products. The excess is left stranded with nothing to react with.

AP Chemistry · Gases

Gas Laws and KMT Lab

Move a piston, heat and add gas, and watch particles hit the walls. Graph the gas laws, compare speed distributions, mix gases and see when real gases stop acting ideal.

AP Chemistry · Acids and Bases

Virtual Titration Simulator

Pick an analyte, a titrant and an indicator, then add titrant drop by drop and watch the pH curve grow. Find the equivalence point, and read the pKa at the half-equivalence point.

Chemistry · Beer's Law

Beer's Law Spectrophotometer Lab

Zero a spectrophotometer, measure standards to build a calibration curve, then measure a random unknown and calculate its concentration from your line.

AP Chemistry · Kinetics

Kinetics and Collision Theory Sandbox

Watch A and B bounce around a box and react only when they hit hard enough. Change the temperature, the concentration and the activation energy, add a catalyst, and see the concentration graph respond.

Physics · Linear Motion

Physics Graphing Lab

Run a cart along a track and watch its position, velocity and acceleration graphs grow together. Sketch your prediction first, then compare it with the model.

Physics · 2D Dynamics

Projectile Motion Vector Lab

Fire a cannon from any height, angle and speed, and turn on live velocity and acceleration vectors. Watch the horizontal velocity stay the same while the vertical velocity shrinks, flips and grows.

Physics · Electric Circuits

Circuit Builder

Drag batteries, wires, resistors, switches and bulbs onto a board and probe the circuit with a multimeter. Switch between series and parallel and see why the bulbs glow so differently.

Test Prep · ACT Science

ACT Science Practice

Practice with original ACT-style science passages, then get an estimated score and tips for the skills you missed.

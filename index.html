Practice Activities

Each activity goes with a lesson on zornscience.org. No login needed, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

Chemistry · Ionic Bonding

Predicting Ion Charge Trainer

Find valence electrons, decide what changes, and check your reasoning with hints and feedback.

Chemistry · Ionic Bonding

Ionic Formula Builder

Turn names into formulas and formulas into names, and check that the charges add to 0.

Chemistry · Reference

Periodic Table

Look up any element, and color the table by atomic radius, electronegativity or ionization energy to see the trends.

Chemistry · Periodic Trends

Periodic Trends Practice

Compare two elements and use the trend to decide which has the larger radius or the higher electronegativity or ionization energy.

Chemistry · Covalent Bonding

Lewis Structure and VSEPR Builder

Count valence electrons, build each Lewis structure, then predict its shape, bond angle and polarity.

Chemistry · Stoichiometry

Stoichiometry Coach

Build conversion factors, watch the units cancel, and solve grams and moles problems with hints and feedback.

AP Chemistry · Gases

Gas Laws and KMT Lab

Move a piston, heat and add gas, and watch particles hit the walls. Graph the gas laws, compare speed distributions, mix gases and see when real gases stop acting ideal.

Physics · Linear Motion

Physics Graphing Lab

Run a cart along a track and watch its position, velocity and acceleration graphs grow together. Sketch your prediction first, then compare it with the model.

Test Prep · ACT Science

ACT Science Practice

Practice with original ACT-style science passages, then get an estimated score and tips for the skills you missed.

About this activity

Estimate hidden meadow and pond populations with quadrats and mark and recapture. Compare random placement, sample size, repeated trials and biased sampling.

Instructions

This is a simulation, not real field data. In each part you will estimate a hidden number, then check how close you were.

Part 1: Count the plants in a few squares of a meadow and estimate the whole meadow. Try picking the squares yourself, then try Random. Click a square to count it, or use arrow keys and Enter.

Part 2: Catch fish, tag them, and release them. Catch a second batch and use the tagged fish you find to estimate the whole pond.

Part 3: See what happens when tags fall off or tagged fish dodge the net. Make a prediction first.

Record your results and answer the questions on your worksheet. Nothing is saved or sent. Reloading the page clears your work.

Use the reef diagram for the pre-lab on pages 1 and 2. If you already finished it, start by measuring the container.

Read the ruler at both ends of the inside edge. Measure the length and width of the plot and of one quadrat. Use the close-up buttons to inspect the aligned ticks.

Draw three different squares at random. Count a ladybug in the square that contains its center. You can turn on center markers and return to any sample.

Use your paper worksheet for all measurements, counts, calculations and answers. Finish your estimate before revealing the actual population.

How to practice

  1. Choose English, Math, or Science. Each subject has 200 ACT-style questions.
  2. Read the question and any passage or data table. Select an answer, then press Check answer to see how you did.
  3. If you’re stuck, press Show answer at any time. Read the explanation to understand the reasoning.
  4. Press Next question for another random question. You can switch subjects whenever you like.

Questions do not repeat within a subject until you’ve seen all 200. Switching subjects keeps your place in each bank during this visit. Reloading the page or opening a shared link starts a new session.

Practice is untimed. No sign-in is required, and scores are not saved. These original skill exercises are separate from the ACT testing platform and are not official ACT questions.

pH & Buffer Lab

Instructions

Explore acids and bases, build a buffer, then add the same acid or base to water and a buffer. Compare the pH changes and find the buffer capacity.

  1. Choose a mode. The pH Explorer compares solutes at different concentrations.
  2. Build a conjugate acid and base pair, then use Stress Test to compare water and buffer.
  3. Add HCl or NaOH to both beakers. Record the curve and download your data.
  4. Use Challenges to test buffer design and indicator choice.

1. Design

Make a buffer at pH X

Pick a pair with pKa within 1 of the target. Set [A⁻]/[HA] to reach the target within 0.05 pH.

2. Maintain

Keep the blood at 7.35 to 7.45

Use H₂CO₃ / HCO₃⁻. Adjust [HCO₃⁻]/[H₂CO₃] to keep the model in the target range.

This is a closed, ideal carbonate model at 25 °C with pKa 6.35. Real blood is an open system regulated by lungs and kidneys, with other buffers and a different temperature.

3. Detect

Pick the indicator

Choose the indicator with pKa closest to the equivalence point. Its transition range is approximately pKa ± 1.

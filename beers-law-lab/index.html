Beer's Law Spectrophotometer Lab

Instructions

Zero the spectrophotometer, measure standards of known concentration to build a calibration curve, then measure an unknown and calculate its concentration.

  1. Zero the instrument with a blank, then measure standards with known concentrations.
  2. Use the calibration curve to find the concentration of the unknown sample.
  3. Record your measurements and explain the relationship between concentration and absorbance.

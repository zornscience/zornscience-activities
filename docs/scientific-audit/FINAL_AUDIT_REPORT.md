# ZornScience scientific accuracy audit

November 19, 2026. Reviewed commit `f59c899e57c8d965abfd247326c3b8ee124a4972` on the repository's `main` branch. Git identifies the audit package's hexadecimal identifier as a **commit**. The audit branch starts at that commit.

The attached inventory and the baseline homepage match on all **76** activity routes. All 76 have a coverage record and received a source, numerical or test review plus a local browser load at three viewport sizes. **0 fully verified, 76 partially verified, 0 not verified.** Activity-by-activity coverage and remaining work are in [ACTIVITY_COVERAGE.md](ACTIVITY_COVERAGE.md).

Across repository and public sources, the audit confirmed **eight scientific defects and one accessibility defect**. The branch addresses seven findings in repository scope. The Iodine Clock PDFs, Snell’s Law answer key and parallel WordPress text still need publication-side corrections. The branch also clarifies the local Iodine Clock measurement and Population Sampling model assumptions.

## Preliminary defect findings

| ID | Classification | Independent evidence and disposition |
|---|---|---|
| ZA-001 | Confirmed defect | Baseline `check()` accepted the hidden 0.175 M outcome with a measured unknown and zero standards. A seeded browser run hit that scoring path, and the model admitted duplicates, flat trends and descending calibrations. A two-standard example with absorbance 0.69, slope 4 M⁻¹ and intercept 0.05 requires 0.160 M even when the simulation's hidden concentration is 0.150 M. The assessment now uses the measured calibration. |
| ZA-002 | Confirmed defect | The activity HTML, shared descriptions and homepage used wording that could mean individual water motion stops, in both languages. The model gives equal positive opposing fluxes at isotonicity. The same wording was reproduced in the web reader on the public activities page and the WordPress Biology index. The branch corrects the repository descriptions. [Public activity](https://activities.zornscience.org/osmosis-lab/) · [Biology index](https://zornscience.org/teaching/virtual-labs/biology/) |
| ZA-003 | Confirmed defect in published PDFs | Both PDFs define reaction rate as reciprocal time without identifying it as a relative metric. The worksheet and key repeat that label in tables and graph prompts, and the key plots it in s⁻¹. At fixed endpoint, `1/t` can compare runs. A concentration-based reaction rate requires an amount per volume per time. The activity itself originally described an inverse-rate proxy and now makes the distinction explicitly in both languages. [Student worksheet](https://zornscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/iodine-clock-virtual-lab.pdf) · [Answer key](https://zornscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/iodine-clock-virtual-lab-key.pdf) |
| ZA-004 | Confirmed pedagogical limitation | The key identifies its emphasized entries as model values. The pre-lab nevertheless presents calculated molarities with 3 significant figures from nominal inputs written as 0.10 M and 2.0, 4.0 or 8.0 mL. The arithmetic gives 0.0125, 0.025 and 0.050 M. Its extra digits are defensible as model guard digits, but the key leaves the distinction from measured precision implicit. The local activity now labels nominal concentrations explicitly. [Answer key](https://zornscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/iodine-clock-virtual-lab-key.pdf) |
| ZA-005 | Confirmed defect in the equilibrium helper | For `N₂ + 3 H₂ ⇌ 2 NH₃` with initial concentrations 0.25, 0.15 and 0 M and K = 0.06, the approximation gives `x = sqrt(0.06 × 0.25 × 0.15³) / 2 ≈ 0.003558 M`. Hydrogen depletion is `100 × 3x / 0.15 ≈ 7.12%`. The baseline reported about 2.37% and accepted the approximation. The current guided presets use unit reactant coefficients, so the ammonia case was reproduced at the helper, not via a student control. The corrected helper and guided explanation use the actual stoichiometric change. |

| Q06 | Requires additional investigation | Unit checks cover water autoionization, charge balance, strong and weak titrations and buffer depletion. | Q07 | Requires additional investigation | Equilibrium extents, common-ion Ksp and ionic reaction mass/charge have executable coverage. The correction in ZA-005 is confirmed. Additional full-range boundary and visual checks are needed before certifying this group. |
| Q08 | Requires additional investigation | The 2.000 g gravimetric samples imply about 1.889 g CaCO₃ for sodium carbonate and 1.448 g for potassium carbonate, both below the calcium-limited maximum at 0.0250 mol calcium. Calorimetry tests check heat conservation and reagent exhaustion. Published material reconciliation remains open. |
| Q09 | Requires additional investigation | The executed tests include 2,000 random one-dimensional collisions and an independent circuit solver comparison across 4,000 random boards. They exercise momentum, kinetic energy in elastic collisions, current law, power balance and opens/shorts. Visual vectors, polarity cases and meter/export agreement need broader comparison. |
| Q10 | Requires additional investigation | Baseline passed seeded outputs and bounds in the biology models. Browser checks exercised osmosis and selected mark-recapture and BTB tests. Biological plausibility, chart animation and export agreement across every visual state remain open. |
| Q11 | Confirmed scientific or pedagogical limitation | `MC/R` is undefined for zero recaptures. Even with random catches, its conditional expectation can differ from N. In a closed pond with N=300, M=C=20, an exact hypergeometric calculation gives about 24.0% zero recaptures and a mean unrounded finite estimate of about 283. Added a bilingual explanation of sensitivity to small R and a worked reference in the teacher notes. |
| Q12 | Requires additional investigation | The baseline exercised seeded quantitative practice with decimal-string references, alternative number syntax and correct or incorrect units. This audit also found and fixed the Unit 8 subtraction precision defect. A full manually reviewed browser feedback matrix for all activity types is pending. |
| Q13 | Requires additional investigation | All 70 routes advertising Spanish loaded in Spanish at Chromebook and mobile widths. Changed copy was tested in both languages where applicable. This establishes language selection and selected terminology, while full scientific translation and accessibility parity require additional review. |
| Q14 | False positive in the latest snapshot for BTB omission and additional language investigation | An earlier web snapshot omitted BTB. The newer snapshot reproduced during the audit includes its activity and lists English and Spanish correctly for BTB. It still describes Population Sampling as English-only, while the local version advertises Spanish and loaded in Spanish. The deployed direct Spanish path could not be verified from the web reader. ## Published materials spot checks

| Activity | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Iodine Clock | Independent room-temperature times for 2.0, 4.0 and 8.0 mL are 64, 32 and 16 s. With volume-weighted mixing and a 45 kJ/mol Arrhenius factor, the 5 °C and 45 °C conditions give 70.29 and 12.17 s, matching the key precision. The worksheet and key share the rate-label defect. [Worksheet](https://zornscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/iodine-clock-virtual-lab.pdf) · [Key](https://zornscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/iodine-clock-virtual-lab-key.pdf) |
| Beer’s Law | From the key’s p. 3 data, (1.429 − 0.240)/(0.300 − 0.050) ≈ 4.76 M⁻¹ and 0.777/4.786 ≈ 0.162 M. The worksheet requires a calibration. The old 4% scoring statement in the key needs revision for the branch behavior. [Worksheet](https://zornscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/chem-beers-law-virtual-lab.pdf) · [Key](https://zornscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/chem-beers-law-virtual-lab-key.pdf) |
| Snell’s Law | Air to glass at 30° gives 19.47° and is displayed as 19.5°. The glass-to-air critical angle is arcsin(1/1.5) ≈ 41.81°. The worksheet and key use angles from the normal. The answer to question 4 needs the qualifier in ZA-009. [Worksheet](https://zornscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/phys-snells-law-virtual-lab.pdf) · [Key](https://zornscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/phys-snells-law-virtual-lab-key.pdf) |
| Population Sampling | The worksheet's area scales by 100 quadrats and its mark-recapture comparison uses the simple class model. Its written and visual instructions were sampled. The published pair was retrieved, with full worked-table and version comparison pending. [Worksheet](https://zornscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/bio-population-sampling-virtual-lab.pdf) · [Key](https://zornscience.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/bio-population-sampling-virtual-lab-key.pdf) |

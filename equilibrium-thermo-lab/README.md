# Chemistry lab chooser

Version: 1.1.0

The previously published combined lab URL remains available as a chooser. The standalone labs are now at `../equilibrium-lab/` and `../calorimetry-lab/`. No simulations run on this page.

## Mexican Spanish

Use `?lang=es` or the shared EN / ES links next to the logo. English is the fallback. The heading, the lab names and descriptions, the back link and the logo's accessible name come from `data.js`. In Spanish the links to both labs and the back link carry `?lang=es`, so students stay in Spanish; in English they keep their relative form.

# Equilibrium & Thermodynamics Lab · v1.0.0

Static AP Chemistry simulation at `equilibrium-thermo-lab/`, with no runtime dependencies, accounts, tracking or outside requests. Use `?embed=1` with the shared embed helper on hosts supporting iframes.

## Equilibrium
N₂O₄(g) ⇌ 2 NO₂(g), ΔH° = +57.2 kJ/mol. Initial amounts: 1 mol N₂O₄ and 0.2 mol NO₂, equilibrated in 5 L at 298 K. Kc(298 K) = 0.150. The model computes equilibrium by atom-conserving reaction extent and uses exponential relaxation (3 simulated seconds) to illustrate response. It does not claim experimental kinetics. Kc varies via constant-enthalpy van ’t Hoff; pressure uses the ideal gas law. Substance removal is clamped at zero. Changes remain available while paused. Graph contains up to 3,600 samples; reset to start another run.

## Calorimetry
Copper, aluminum or iron in water, or strong HCl/NaOH neutralization. Insulated system, negligible cup heat capacity; solution density 1 g/mL and specific heat 4.184 J/(g·°C). Neutralization enthalpy −57.3 kJ/mol of water formed. Final temperatures follow energy conservation; exponential traces are illustrative and reach final values at 40 simulated seconds. Editing conditions resets the experiment. Download current lab data as CSV or print the selected lab.

## Assignment ideas
Predict → disturb → explain with Qc/Kc. Compare heating with compression. For calorimetry, compare metal masses or use unequal acid/base amounts to identify the limiting reagent. These open investigations do not issue completion codes.

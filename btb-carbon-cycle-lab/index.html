Does a plant add or remove carbon dioxide?

1. Prepare the indicator

Model details

This is an illustrative teaching model. Real spinach and real BTB vary. The colors and times are examples for comparing tubes, not predictions of exact lab results.

CO2 and water make carbonic acid, so more CO2 makes the water more acidic. BTB is yellow when acidic, green near neutral, and blue when basic.

Spinach respires all the time and adds CO2. In the light, photosynthesis also uses CO2. The tube changes in the direction of whichever process is stronger.

Instructions

Blow through the straw until the BTB is green. Set up three tubes, predict each color after 24 hours, then watch the time-lapse.

Predict first. Compare colors on Day 1 and Day 2. Your results are not saved or sent.

The color is never the only clue. Each tube also has a color name and a CO2 bar. Turn on the pattern aid if colors are hard to tell apart.

Record your hypothesis, variables, and conclusion on your worksheet. Nothing is saved or sent. Your data stays on this page until you reload or change language.

## Independent worked answers

These answers were worked before authoring the bank. Original practice is aligned to the College Board CED topics named below, not copied from official exam items. Concentrations approximate activities for ideal dilute solutions; gases are ideal. All equilibrium constants and quotients use the dimensionless convention in this activity. Concentrations are in M, pressures in atm, temperature in kelvin. Every reaction uses ⇌. Temperature is fixed unless a question explicitly changes it. Counts in particle models are exact; measured data determine the stated precision.

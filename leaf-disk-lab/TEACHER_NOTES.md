# Teacher notes

Version: 1.0.0

Use the lab with ninth grade Biology. Ask students to compare one factor at a
time, use both tables and graphs, and explain a claim with evidence. Short
frames in English and Mexican Spanish support students who are learning either
language. Students can copy all tables and writing into Google Classroom.
Nothing is saved or sent by the activity. Remind them to copy before leaving,
reloading or switching language. There is no completion code.

Suggested sequence: model air removal together, compare water with bicarbonate,
then assign the light color, brightness and temperature investigations. Finish
with a student-designed test and the three check questions. A fixed ?seed=42
link gives the same disk thresholds in every trial. Without a seed, discuss
variation between ten-disk samples. A fixed seed is helpful for comparing only
the independent variable; different seeds are helpful for discussing repeats.

## Misconceptions

| Idea to challenge | Observation and explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Green light is best because leaves are green | Green still works, but more slowly. Leaves reflect much of that light. |
| Carbon dioxide makes the disks float | Bicarbonate supplies carbon dioxide that the leaf uses. Oxygen produced by photosynthesis accumulates and supports floating. |
| Hotter always means faster | 35 °C is slower than 25 °C; at 45 °C no disk floats in this model. |
| Plants only respire at night | Respiration uses oxygen with light and in darkness. Dark trials make no oxygen through photosynthesis. |

The response measure is minutes until 5 of 10 disks float. "none by 45 min"
means that five disks have not floated within the observation window, not a
measured time of zero or 45. Compare the whole count curve as well as that
summary. Ten disks are a small sample. Individual disks can differ.

## Limits and review

This is an illustrative teaching model. It is not calibrated to empirical
spinach measurements. Developer constants are Rmax = 1, median oxygen threshold
3.7740985308779993 and lognormal sigma 0.2. Independent lognormal thresholds
give roughly five floating disks at 8 min under default conditions. Green is
about 4.12 times slower with the same thresholds. The model does not address
photosystems, the Calvin cycle or detailed enzyme kinetics. The explanation
that enzymes stop working at 45 °C is a simplified teaching statement required
for this version; real effects depend on tissue and exposure.

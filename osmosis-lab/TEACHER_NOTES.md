# Teacher notes

Version: 1.0.0

About 40 to 50 minutes. Students need their worksheet or a document open to paste their data and write.

Before class: open the lab once and try the bench. The hidden isotonic point changes on every page load
(0.25 to 0.35 M), so neighbors get slightly different answers and cannot copy a number.

Talking points:

- Why weigh before soaking? You need a starting mass to find a change.
- Why percent change, not grams? The cores start at different masses.
- Why blot? Solution on the surface is not water that moved into the cells. Students who skip it see a hint
  and can reweigh.
- Why three trials? Each core is a little different. Averages are more reliable.
- At the isotonic point water still moves. The zoom view shows crossings both ways with no net flow.

What gets copied: the prediction, every measurement, averages, the estimated isotonic point and three sentence
starters. Percent changes are copied only where the student has shown them or turned on auto-calculate, so you can
ask students to calculate them in Sheets instead.

Nothing is saved or sent. Reloading clears the work, so remind students to copy their data before closing the tab.

This is an illustrative model. Real potatoes vary, real cores often level off at high concentrations, and real
isotonic points depend on the potato.

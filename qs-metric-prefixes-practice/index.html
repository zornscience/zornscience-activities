    Reference

    The prefix ladder

    Rungs from top to bottom

    • M, mega: 106
    • k, kilo: 103
    • Base unit (m, g or L): 100
    • c, centi: 10−2
    • m, milli: 10−3
    • μ, micro: 10−6
    • n, nano: 10−9

    Moving the decimal point

    • The number of places between two rungs is the difference of their exponents. Kilo to centi is 3 − (−2) = 5 places.
    • Down the ladder to a smaller unit, move the decimal point right. Smaller unit, bigger number: 1.5 km = 1500 m.
    • Up the ladder to a larger unit, move it left: 325 mg = 0.325 g.
    • Keep the zeros at the end, so the significant figures stay the same: 25.0 mL = 0.0250 L.
    • Capital M is mega; lowercase m is milli. μ is the Greek letter mu.

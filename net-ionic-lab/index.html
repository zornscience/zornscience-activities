How to use this lab

  1. Select a mixture from the reagent shelf.
  2. Inspect the starting solutions, then choose Mix reagents. Watch the glassware as the reagents combine.
  3. Choose Let it settle to distinguish suspended solid from clear solution. For the zinc trial, observe the coating on the metal.
  4. Reset to replay, or select another mixture. Open Reveal the chemistry after making your own observations.

The ten mixtures follow the attached lab’s observation table. Solution colors and precipitate textures are illustrative; actual appearance depends on lighting, concentration and time. Mixing colors alone does not establish a reaction.

Virtual reagents only. In a real lab, use eye protection and your teacher’s handling and waste instructions, especially for lead and silver compounds and sodium hydroxide.

    Bonding reference

    Valence electrons: H 1, B 3, C 4, N 5, O 6, F 7, Cl 7. Add one electron for each negative charge; subtract one for each positive charge.

    Formal charge = valence electrons − nonbonding electrons − half the bonding electrons. Report formal charge in units of e, the elementary charge.

    Coulomb's law: attraction magnitude is proportional to the charge product divided by distance squared. A potential energy curve has its equilibrium bond length at the minimum.

    Each single, double, or triple bond counts as one electron domain. Include lone pairs when finding electron geometry.

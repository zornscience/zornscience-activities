    Exponent and log rules

    • Multiplying powers of ten adds the exponents: 103 × 104 = 107
    • Dividing subtracts them, top minus bottom: 102 ÷ 105 = 10−3
    • A power of a power multiplies them: (103)2 = 106
    • 100 = 1. A negative exponent means a number between 0 and 1, not a negative number.
    • Scientific notation: multiply or divide the coefficients, add or subtract the exponents, then adjust so the coefficient is at least 1 and less than 10.
    • log asks: 10 to what power? The log of a power of ten is its exponent, and numbers below 1 have negative logs: log(1000) = 3, log(0.001) = −3
    • pH = −log[H+]
    • Undo the log with 10ˣ (2nd, LOG): [H+] = 10−pH
    • Significant figures in [H⁺] = decimal places in the pH.

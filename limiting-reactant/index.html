Limiting Reactant Visualizer

Instructions

Choose how many moles of each reactant to start with, predict which one runs out, then watch the molecules pair up into products. Whatever is left over has nothing to react with.

  1. Choose the reaction and starting amounts, then predict the limiting reactant.
  2. Run the reaction and compare the particles with the before, change, and after table.
  3. Identify the excess reactant and explain what limits the amount of product.

    Solubility rules for 4.2

    • For dilute aqueous solutions near room temperature: Group 1, ammonium and nitrate salts are soluble.
    • Chlorides are generally soluble except those of Ag, Pb and Hg(I). Sulfates are generally soluble except those of Ba, Sr and Pb. CaSO4: limited solubility.
    • Carbonates and phosphates are generally insoluble except Group 1 and ammonium salts. Hydroxides are generally insoluble except Group 1; Ca, Sr and Ba hydroxides are more soluble. These are guidelines; use any specific data in a prompt.

    Keep weak acids, solids and pure liquids intact in ionic equations. Separate soluble strong electrolytes into ions and cancel only unchanged spectator ions.

    Molarity = moles / liters of solution. Use balanced coefficients for mole ratios. Percent yield = actual yield / theoretical yield × 100.

      Instructions
      

      Watch a population of beetles across generations. Beetles that match the ground are harder for birds to see. Survivors have offspring that inherit their shell color. A rare random change can add a different color to the offspring.
      

      Choose your prediction, then start a run. Step one generation at a time or run to the last generation. Settings lock during a run. In Investigation 3, start each new run with fresh beetles.
      

      Dark beetles have solid oval shells. Light beetles have striped, pointed shells. The ground also has a texture. Every drawing has counts and a data table.
      

      This is an illustrative teaching model, not real data. Real populations have many traits and changing conditions.
      

      Copy the tables and prompts to your worksheet or document. Write your answers there. Nothing is saved or sent. Reloading or changing language clears your work.

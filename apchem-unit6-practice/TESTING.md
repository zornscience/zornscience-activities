These solutions were worked before writing the question bank. Enthalpies in kJ/mol refer to one mole of reaction as written unless a named substance is specified. Heat q is an extensive quantity in J or kJ. All calorimetry models neglect heat loss and calorimeter heat capacity unless stated.

    Heat and reaction enthalpy

    q = mcΔT; ΔT = Tfinal − Tinitial.

    Specific heat capacity of water: 4.18 J/(g·°C).

    At constant pressure with only pressure-volume work, system heat equals its enthalpy change. For an insulated calorimeter with negligible cup heat capacity:

    qreaction = −qsolution; ΔH = qreaction/nreaction.

    ΔH°rxn = Σ ΔH°f(products) − Σ ΔH°f(reactants).

    ΔH = Σ bonds broken − Σ bonds formed.

    Every sum includes balanced coefficients. ΔH values in kJ/mol refer to one mole of reaction as written, unless a named substance is specified. Heat q is in J or kJ.

    The standard enthalpy of formation of an element in its standard state is 0.

    Phase changes of water

    q = nΔHphase. Fusion: +6.01 kJ/mol. Vaporization: +40.7 kJ/mol.

    Phase values apply at the normal melting and boiling points, respectively. They are not the difference between the 298 K gas and liquid formation values.

How to use this lab

Compare the characteristic flame colors of eight known metal salts, then identify an unknown sample.

  1. Explore the known samples. Drag a bottle onto the Bunsen burner. On a touchscreen or keyboard, tap or activate a bottle to test it.
  2. Observe and compare. Watch the flame and record your notes. The virtual wire loop is automatically cleaned between tests.
  3. Investigate the unknown. Its identity is one of the eight known salts. Compare its color, choose the metal ion, and check your prediction.

Heating excites atoms and ions in the sample. As electrons return to lower energy levels, they emit characteristic wavelengths of light. Flame tests provide clues; similar colors may need another method to confirm an identity.

This simulation uses separate, pure samples and idealized colors. Sodium contamination can mask other colors in a real lab. Follow your teacher’s safety instructions for physical experiments.
